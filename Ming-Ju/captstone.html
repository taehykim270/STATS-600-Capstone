Capstone Project -- Weather Prediction¶

You will be provided a dataset that contains daily weather conditions at 98 airports over a ten year period, January 1 2005 -- December 31 2014.

The 98 airports are:

ABQ AIY ALB AMA ATL AUS BFL BHM BIS BMG BNA BOS BTR BUF BWI BZN CHA CHS CLE CLL CLT CMI COS CRW CVG DEN DFW DLH DSM DTW EKO ELP EUG EYW FAR FLG FLL FNT FOE FSD GEG GJT GNV GRB GRR HFD HOU IAD ILG IND LAN LAS LAX LCK LGA LIT LNK MAF MCI MCO MEM MIA MKE MOB MOT MSN MSP MYR OAK OKC OLM OMA ORD PDX PHL PHX PIT PVD RAP RDU RFD RIC RNO SAN SAT SAV SDF SEA SFO SLC STL SUX TLH TTN TUL TUS TVC UNV

Your goal is to build models to predict

  • daily max temperature
  • daily mean temperature
  • daily min temperature

at the following 15 airports

  • ABQ
  • ATL
  • BOS
  • DEN
  • DFW
  • DLH
  • DTW
  • EYW
  • IAD
  • LAN
  • LAX
  • SEA
  • SFO
  • SLC
  • TVC

for 1--5 days into the future.

More specifically, once you have completed building your predictive models, you will be given data from a "validation" year (a year other than 2005--2014). For each month in the validation year, you will be given data from days 1--15 of that month and will be asked to predict temperatures for days 16--20. Thus, in total you will make (3 temperatures) $\times$ (15 airports) $\times$ (12 months) $\times$ (5 days) = 2700 predictions. Your goal is to minimize the MSE of your predictions.

You will complete the project in groups of 3 or 4. You can use any methods / models covered in this course. Your code must be finalized and shared via github before you are given the validation data. You will give a short (10 minute) presentation in class on the approach you used. Your presentation should include:

  • Any EDA you found particularly relevant
    (remember, everyone has the same data, so no need to give a general overview)
  • A description of how / why you chose your final models
  • A description of your final models
  • An evaluation of your models, and your expected MSE
    (perhaps broken down by airport, or by time of year, or by prediction day (1--5)
  • Anything else you find relevant / interesting

You will also submit a brief (3 page max) written report describing your work (which may reference figures in the slides). The report should also include the final performance on the validation data, and brief discussion of what went right/wrong.

Timeline:

  • 11/3: Form groups
  • 12/2: Code finalized
  • 12/3: Presentations in class
  • 12/3: Validation data released
  • 12/7: Deadline to submit predictions (by noon, please)
  • 12/10: Written report due
