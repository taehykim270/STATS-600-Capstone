The shared model note is one folder up at ../airport_temperature_model_note.pdf.

baseline/ is the featured ordinary Lasso with 1,669 candidate coefficients per fit. mean_only/ is the matched 699-input ordinary Lasso. Both directories contain temperature_scores.csv and mse_by_lead_target.csv. Details include errors by target date and forecast window. Artifacts include fitted models, signed nonzero coefficients and individual CV and evaluation forecasts. The featured processed matrix is included in the local project and can be regenerated with preprocess_weather.R. The distribution bundle includes the four raw input fields per station.

experiments/model_scores.csv reproduces every row of the score table, including the day-15 subset of the featured fit. by_lead.csv, by_target.csv and by_lead_target.csv give the corresponding stratified scores. external_temperature_* files pair the two spatial temperature designs. The signed difference always equals all three minus mean only. *_selection_* and *_selected_families.csv count nonzero coefficients from the final fits. lambda_cv.csv reports the complete tuning grid. loss_by_fit_fold_and_penalty.csv gives counts and squared-error sums by airport, lead, target, fit end year and candidate model or penalty. The models/ subdirectory stores the other fitted regressions by target airport with their preprocessing and PCA rotations. The model IDs match model_key.csv.

Temperature errors are squared degrees Fahrenheit. Missing observed targets are excluded from the denominator. Pooled scores are weighted by counts. All initial 2005-2012 fits and expanding-window validation use complete five-input/five-target calendar windows, with missing predictors filled from training data. Every fit uses temperature and pressure as its only weather measurements. No coefficient penalty depends on distance.
